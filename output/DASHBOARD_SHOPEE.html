📊 Analisa Profitabilitas — Sesuai Standar Profesional Marketplace

Formula mengacu: Shopee Ads Help Center (Platform ROAS = attributed revenue / ad spend), DataGlass True ROAS Methodology 2026, Bain e-Conomy SEA 2025, Shopee Fee Schedule 4 Aug 2026. True ROAS = (Penghasilan − HPP) / Ad Spend. Break-even ROAS = 1 / Margin Rate. Margin Rate = (Penghasilan − HPP) / Subtotal.

Ad Spend diambil dari Beban Manual yang namanya mengandung iklan/ads/promosi per akun & periode. Bisa edit manual di atas.

KPI Analisa — ROI / ROAS / TACOS

True ROAS = Contribution / Ad Spend. Platform ROAS (Shopee) = Subtotal / Ad Spend (gross, tanpa HPP/fees). Break-even = 1 / Margin Rate. TACOS = Ad Spend / Subtotal. ROI Ads = (True ROAS − 1) × 100%. Net Margin = Laba Bersih / Subtotal.
